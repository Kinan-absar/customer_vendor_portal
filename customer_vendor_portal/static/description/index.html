Customer & Vendor Portal Extension

A practical vendor-facing portal for Purchase Order visibility, invoice submission, review status and account onboarding without backend access.

Vendor Portal Purchase Orders Invoice Upload Review Workflow Portal Security

Vendor Self-Service

Reduce email follow-ups by giving vendor portal users a dedicated workspace for their Purchase Orders and invoice submissions.

Smart First Login

Vendor onboarding tracks the first portal visit so account details can be completed before the user moves into the normal vendor dashboard experience.

Vendor Dashboard

Dedicated vendor home with invoice and status information relevant to the logged-in supplier.

Purchase Order Visibility

Vendors can browse their Purchase Orders and open detailed order information without receiving backend access.

PO-linked Invoice Upload

Upload an invoice document with vendor invoice number, date, amount and notes and associate it with the relevant Purchase Order.

Duplicate Invoice Protection

A vendor invoice number is unique per vendor, helping prevent accidental duplicate submissions.

Review Workflow

Track Submitted, Under Review, Approved and Rejected states. Internal reviewers can publish a review comment that the vendor sees in the portal.

Internal Review Activities

New vendor submissions can schedule activities for authorized reviewer users, keeping the accounting follow-up visible inside Odoo.

Secure Attachments

Invoice files are stored against the vendor invoice record and can be downloaded from the controlled workflow.
